Baltic Power

The largest green energy project in Central Europe

Baltic Power, a joint venture of ORLEN and Northland Power, has reached the most advanced development phase among all offshore wind projects in Poland and is the only one to obtain FID. Its onshore infrastructure and key packages are being constructed, while offshore installation started in February 2025. The 76 turbines, each with 15 MW capacity, will cover approximately 3% of Poland’s electricity demand — the daily needs of 1.5 million households.
